# Attune Design Contract

Attune should feel like quiet intelligence: a private, warm editorial notebook rather than a corporate dashboard or romantic social network.

The primary interaction is natural-language capture. The person describes what happened; Attune visibly files distinct candidates into memory, calendar, task, reminder, or interaction. It never asks the person to choose a destination before writing. Every candidate exposes its provenance, confidence language, and rationale, and can be approved or rejected independently.

The visual system uses warm parchment, paper, ink, moss, and restrained amber. Cards use hairline borders, modest radii, almost no shadow, and generous spacing. There are no scores, charts, gradients, or gamified relationship metrics.

Desktop uses a compact left navigation, focused conversation canvas, and small context rail. Mobile becomes a full-width conversation with bottom navigation. Content stays readable at 320px and controls maintain a minimum 44px target.

Facts and inferences are always labeled in text, not color alone. Suggestions use careful language, particularly around sleep, mood, conflict, and internal state. Motion is subtle and respects reduced-motion preferences.
